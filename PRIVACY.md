# Privacy policy: icloud-gcal-sync

Last updated: 2026-11-10

icloud-gcal-sync is a personal tool used by one person, its owner, on their own computer.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's permission, it reads events from iCloud calendars the owner selects, and it creates and edits events in one Google Calendar that the tool created. It requests only the Google scope `calendar.app.created`.

**Where data goes.** Event data moves directly between Apple's iCloud servers, the owner's computer and Google Calendar. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party. No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.

**What is stored.** Google sign-in tokens and settings are kept in files on the owner's computer. The Apple app-specific password is kept in the macOS Keychain. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the developer.

**Sharing.** Data is not sold, shared or used for any purpose other than copying the owner's own calendar events.

**Control.** The owner can stop the tool, delete its local files, remove the tool's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and revoke the Apple password at account.apple.com at any time.
